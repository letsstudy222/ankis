# Evolution art: selected style A, sample approval

This is an art preview only. Existing gameplay stages, collection state and live creature graphics are unchanged. The owner selected style A from the previous three fox concepts and requested examples before the full collection is redrawn.

## References consulted

- [PokeAPI species data](https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi/blob/master/data/v2/csv/pokemon_species.csv) records species identity, a parent species and an evolution-chain ID. The inspected examples include Tauros (a one-species chain), Rattata/Raticate (two species), and Bulbasaur/Ivysaur/Venusaur (three species).
- [PokeAPI evolution data](https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi/blob/master/data/v2/csv/pokemon_evolution.csv) separates progression triggers/conditions from the species identity; inspected examples include level-triggered transitions. This is community-maintained Pokemon data, not a claim that this app implements official Pokemon rules.
- [Pokemanki](https://github.com/zjosua/Pokemanki/blob/master/README.md) describes collecting a creature or egg for started decks, and leveling/evolving it through learning and review. This supports making visual changes recognizable milestones in study progress.
- [Tuxemon](https://github.com/Tuxemon/Tuxemon/blob/development/README.md) is an open-source monster-collecting RPG, consulted as another example of an original monster roster. Its source art/code has not been reused.

The owner's requested 1/2/3 forms by rarity is a new rule for this app; these references do not establish that Pokemon rarity determines evolution count. No existing game character artwork has been copied.

## Proposed form counts

- Normal: one complete form after hatching, with no additional evolution.
- Rare/Epic: two forms total, with one evolution.
- Legend/Rainbow: three forms total, with two evolutions.

Eggs do not count as forms. Exact level/XP conditions will be handled when implementing the approved collection, rather than imposed by an art sample.

## Six sample illustrations for review

1. **Nebula Fox / Hồ Ly Tinh Vân, Normal:** one form close to selected A. Purple/indigo fur, cream muzzle/chest, oversized ears, cyan comet-tail tip and gold forehead star.
2. **Galaxy Axolotl / Kỳ Nhông Ngân Hà, Rare/Epic:** two forms. A compact hatchling develops a longer body, more branched gills, back fins and a flowing tail. Pink/cyan coloration, face and forehead mark stay recognizable.
3. **Prism Star Dragon / Tinh Long Vạn Sắc, Legend/Rainbow:** three forms. Horn buds and tiny wing nubs become functional intermediate wings, then a full wingspan, horn crest, chest mane and layered comet-tail fins. Purple/cyan/cream identity and star marking persist.

Across forms, anatomical development and silhouette changes matter more than merely making the same art larger. Base forms use fewer structural details; later forms add meaningful wings/gills/horns/tail layers. The selected A language remains thick dark outlines, bright color blocking, expressive chibi proportions and crisp cel shading. This sample is rendered as a high-quality design board; production sprites still need individual cutouts and consistent framing after review.
